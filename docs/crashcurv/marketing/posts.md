## X (English)
mPainter 0.9.0 alpha is out. A 3D texture painter built on Blender: layer painting, geometry-based Pointiness curvature and GPU-assisted AO/thickness baking, 10 mask generators and 47 CC0 materials from Poly Haven. Build your own materials with Blender nodes and use them as presets. Linux first. Alpha buyers get 1.0 free. [link]

## Mastodon
mPainter 0.9.0 alpha: a texture painter that is a branch of Blender, GPL v3. Layer painting, Pointiness curvature from subdivided, displaced geometry, AO and thickness baking, mask generators, 47 built-in CC0 materials (thanks @polyhaven). Bring your own Blender node materials as presets. Linux AppImage now, Windows and macOS later. Alpha includes free updates to 1.0. [link] #Blender #b3d #gamedev #texturing #FOSS
